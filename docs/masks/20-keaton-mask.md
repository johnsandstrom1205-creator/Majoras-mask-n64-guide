**Type:** Quest  
**Location:** Kafei's hideout, Laundry Pool

## How to obtain it

Receive it from the Curiosity Shop owner when he visits Kafei's hideout.

1. Complete the Anju/Kafei steps through delivering the Pendant.
2. Enter the Laundry Pool hideout on the Final Day.
3. Take the mask and Special Delivery from the shop owner.

## What it does

Summons Keaton at moving-grass circles; answer five questions for a Piece of Heart.
