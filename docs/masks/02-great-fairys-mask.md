**Type:** Utility  
**Location:** Great Fairy Fountain, North Clock Town

## How to obtain it

Return Clock Town's Stray Fairy to the Great Fairy as human Link.

1. Find the fairy in the Laundry Pool by day or East Clock Town at night.
2. Carry it to the North Clock Town fountain as human Link.

## What it does

Shimmers when a dungeon fairy remains in the room and attracts released Stray Fairies.
