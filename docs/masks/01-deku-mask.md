**Type:** Transformation  
**Location:** Clock Tower interior

## How to obtain it

Play the Song of Healing for the Happy Mask Salesman after recovering the Ocarina and returning to the First Day.

1. Recover the Ocarina from Skull Kid at midnight on the Final Day.
2. Play the Song of Time to reset.
3. Speak to the Happy Mask Salesman and play the Song of Healing.

## What it does

Transforms Link into Deku Link: water hopping, Deku Flowers, bubbles, and a spin attack.
