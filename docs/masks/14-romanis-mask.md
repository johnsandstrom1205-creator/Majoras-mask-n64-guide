**Type:** Access  
**Location:** Cremia's wagon

## How to obtain it

Protect Cremia's milk bottles during the shortcut ambush.

1. Open Romani Ranch on the First Day and defend the barn from 2:00–5:30 AM.
2. Meet Cremia by the barn at 6:00 PM on the Second Day.
3. Shoot both masked pursuers until the wagon reaches Clock Town.

## What it does

Grants entry to the Milk Bar, Latte, after 10:00 PM.
