**Type:** Utility  
**Location:** North Clock Town

## How to obtain it

Stop Sakon from stealing the Bomb Shop owner's mother's bag.

1. Wait near the playground before midnight.
2. Strike Sakon once after he grabs the bag—do not shoot the explosive sack.
3. Speak to the rescued old woman.

## What it does

Creates an explosion at Link's position; raise the shield before detonation to avoid damage.
