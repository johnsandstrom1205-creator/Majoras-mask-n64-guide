**Type:** Quest  
**Location:** Gorman Track, Milk Road

## How to obtain it

Beat the Gorman Brothers in their horse race.

1. Summon Epona at the track.
2. Pay for the race and cut inside the brothers without hitting fences.
3. Finish first.

## What it does

Reveals Garo spirits and convinces Ikana's cliff watcher to create a Hookshot tree.
