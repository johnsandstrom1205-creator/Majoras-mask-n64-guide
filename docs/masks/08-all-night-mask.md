**Type:** Quest  
**Location:** Curiosity Shop, West Clock Town

## How to obtain it

Buy it from the Curiosity Shop for 500 rupees.

1. Prevent the robbery at midnight on the First Night.
2. Withdraw 500 rupees from the bank on the Final Night.
3. Buy the mask after 10:00 PM.

## What it does

Prevents sleep and unlocks both of Anju's Grandmother's story rewards.
