**Type:** Utility  
**Location:** Swamp Spider House

## How to obtain it

Collect all 30 Gold Skulltula Spirits in the Swamp Spider House.

1. Burn the web over the entrance.
2. Search every room, using bugs on soft-soil patches and beans where needed.
3. Return with 30 tokens and speak to the cursed man.

## What it does

Lets Link read Gossip Stones and understand animals, including dogs at Mamamu Yan's race.
