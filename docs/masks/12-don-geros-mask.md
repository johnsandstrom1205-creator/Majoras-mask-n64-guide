**Type:** Quest  
**Location:** Mountain Village

## How to obtain it

Deliver the Rock Sirloin from the Goron Shrine chandelier to the hungry Goron.

1. Light every Goron Shrine torch to spin the chandelier.
2. Roll from the baby's room ramp and break chandelier pots until the sirloin drops.
3. Carry it to Mountain Village and throw it onto the hungry Goron's ledge.

## What it does

Recruits five frogs for the spring choir, which rewards a Piece of Heart.
