**Type:** Transformation  
**Location:** The Moon

## How to obtain it

Clear all four Moon mini-dungeons and surrender all 20 normal masks, then speak to the child wearing Majora's Mask.

1. Bring every mask to the Moon.
2. Complete the Odolwa, Goht, Gyorg, and Twinmold trials while giving away requested masks.
3. Speak to the final child beneath the tree.

## What it does

Transforms Link into Fierce Deity during boss rooms; sword beams make most bosses much easier.
