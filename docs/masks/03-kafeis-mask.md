**Type:** Quest  
**Location:** Mayor's Residence, East Clock Town

## How to obtain it

Accept Madame Aroma's request to find Kafei.

1. Enter the right-hand room in the Mayor's Residence.
2. Speak to Madame Aroma and accept the assignment.

## What it does

Starts the Anju and Kafei investigation and changes dialogue with many townspeople.
