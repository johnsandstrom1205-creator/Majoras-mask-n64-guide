**Type:** Utility  
**Location:** Road to Ikana stone circle

## How to obtain it

Reveal the invisible soldier Shiro and give him Red Potion.

1. Ride over the Ikana fences.
2. Use the Lens at the ring of stones to find Shiro.
3. Speak to him and give him Red Potion.

## What it does

Makes most ordinary guards and enemies ignore Link; especially useful in Pirates' Fortress.
