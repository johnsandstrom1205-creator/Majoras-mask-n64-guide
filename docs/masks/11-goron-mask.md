**Type:** Transformation  
**Location:** Goron Graveyard

## How to obtain it

Follow Darmani's ghost and heal him at his grave.

1. Use the Lens of Truth to see Darmani in Goron Village.
2. Follow him to the invisible ladder in Mountain Village.
3. Play the Song of Healing at the grave.

## What it does

Transforms Link into Goron Link: rolling, punching, pounding, lava resistance, and heavy switches.
