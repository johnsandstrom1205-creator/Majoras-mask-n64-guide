**Type:** Combat  
**Location:** Stone Tower Temple

## How to obtain it

Defeat the late Eyegore guarding its chest in the N64 temple route.

1. Advance through the inverted temple after obtaining Light Arrows.
2. Bait the Eyegore's attack and shoot its exposed eye.
3. Open the resulting chest.

## What it does

Turns Link giant during Twinmold's battle and continuously drains magic.
