**Type:** Access  
**Location:** Music Box House, Ikana Canyon

## How to obtain it

Sneak into Pamela's house and heal her transformed father.

1. Play Song of Storms in the river cave to restart the Music Box House.
2. Enter while Pamela is outside.
3. Approach the basement wardrobe and play Song of Healing.

## What it does

Makes Gibdos and many ReDeads non-hostile; required for the well's item trades.
