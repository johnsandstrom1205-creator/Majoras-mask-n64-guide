**Type:** Utility  
**Location:** Deku Palace race tunnel

## How to obtain it

Follow the Deku Butler through his timed underground course.

1. Return the princess to the throne room.
2. Enter the side cave outside the palace.
3. Keep pace with the Butler; use Bunny Hood for human sections and Deku Link where required.

## What it does

Reveals fragrant mushrooms used to brew Blue Potion.
