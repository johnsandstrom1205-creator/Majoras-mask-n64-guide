**Type:** Quest  
**Location:** East Clock Town

## How to obtain it

Give the Special Delivery to the Postman, follow him to Latte, then speak to him after delivery.

1. Give the letter directly to the Postman instead of Madame Aroma.
2. Wait while he delivers it at the Milk Bar.
3. Speak to him outside when he has been released from duty.

## What it does

Allows Link to inspect any Clock Town mailbox; the first check awards a Piece of Heart.
