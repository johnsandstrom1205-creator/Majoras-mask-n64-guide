**Type:** Utility

## How to obtain it

Listen to Guru-Guru's confession at the Laundry Pool.

1. Visit Guru-Guru on the bench at night.
2. Speak until his full story ends.

## What it does

Makes small animals march behind Link; required to grow Grog's ten Cucco chicks.
