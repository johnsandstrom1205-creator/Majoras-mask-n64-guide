**Type:** Quest  
**Location:** Milk Bar, Latte

## How to obtain it

Complete Toto's sound check in all four forms.

1. Enter Latte wearing Romani's Mask.
2. Perform at Toto's marked spot as human, Deku, Goron, and Zora Link.
3. Speak to Gorman after the completed song.

## What it does

Calms the Gorman Brothers during Cremia's ambush; mainly a story and collection mask on N64.
