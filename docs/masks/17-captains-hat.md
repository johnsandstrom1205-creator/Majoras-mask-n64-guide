**Type:** Quest  
**Location:** Ikana Graveyard

## How to obtain it

Wake, chase, and defeat Skull Keeta, then open his chest.

1. Play the Sonata at the giant skeleton.
2. Stun him with arrows while defeating Stalchildren to catch up.
3. Defeat him, then Hookshot or jump to the chest.

## What it does

Lets Link command Stalchildren and open the three night-specific graves.
