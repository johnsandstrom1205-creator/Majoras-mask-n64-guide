**Type:** Transformation  
**Location:** Great Bay Coast

## How to obtain it

Push the injured Mikau to shore and heal his spirit.

1. Swim behind Mikau and push him to the beach.
2. Listen to his final song and play the Song of Healing.

## What it does

Transforms Link into Zora Link: fast swimming, underwater walking, fin boomerangs, guitar, and electric barrier.
