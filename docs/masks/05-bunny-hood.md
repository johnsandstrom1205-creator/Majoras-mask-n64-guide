**Type:** Utility  
**Location:** Cucco Shack, Romani Ranch

## How to obtain it

March with all ten chicks until each becomes an adult Cucco, then speak to Grog.

1. Wear the Bremen Mask and hold B to march.
2. Touch all ten chicks without ending the march.
3. Keep marching until all ten grow, then talk to Grog.

## What it does

Greatly increases running speed and keeps the Postman's ten-second timer visible.
