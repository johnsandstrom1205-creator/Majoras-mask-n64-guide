**Type:** Quest  
**Location:** Stock Pot Inn employees-only room

## How to obtain it

Wait with Anju until Kafei returns and exchanges the Sun and Moon Masks.

1. Recover the Sun's Mask in Sakon's Hideout.
2. Return to the Stock Pot Inn employees-only room.
3. Wait through the final minutes until Kafei arrives.

## What it does

Ends the mayor's meeting and awards a Piece of Heart; required for full notebook completion.
