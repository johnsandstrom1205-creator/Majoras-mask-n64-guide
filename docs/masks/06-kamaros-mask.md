**Type:** Quest  
**Location:** Northern Termina Field mushroom stones

## How to obtain it

Heal the ghost dancer Kamaro.

1. Climb the mushroom-shaped stone where music can be heard after midnight.
2. Speak to Kamaro and play the Song of Healing.

## What it does

Teaches Kamaro's dance; performing it for the Rosa Sisters awards a Piece of Heart.
