Every temple contains 15 Stray Fairies. The Great Fairy's Mask shimmers when the current room still contains an uncollected fairy and attracts a fairy after its bubble or container is broken.

| Temple | Common hiding patterns | Great Fairy reward |
|---|---|---|
| Woodfall | Entrance platforms, defeated enemies, beehives, pots, central-room switches, and bubbles in the final flower chamber | Enhanced Spin Attack |
| Snowhead | Invisible Lens alcoves, Freezards, crates, floor switches, Dinolfos, and a hidden ledge in the central tower | Double Magic |
| Great Bay | Torches, Skulltula/barrel/pot drops, underwater containers, the Ice Arrow room, waterwheel alcoves, and the final green-pipe chamber | Double Defense |
| Stone Tower | Sun emblems, Armos/Eyegore/Wizzrobe drops, lava and wind rooms, and chests that require switching between normal and inverted temple states | Great Fairy's Sword |

## Safe collection method

1. Slow time before entering.
2. Clear each room fully before taking the next key door.
3. Put on the Great Fairy's Mask at both entrances of every room.
4. Check the dungeon map: a fairy icon confirms that a room's fairy or fairies have been collected.
5. Return all 15 to the regional fountain **before** resetting time.

## N64 warning

The 3DS remake moved several fairies. Do not use a 3DS room-by-room list with this guide. This site follows the N64 layout.
