The original Nintendo 64 game has exactly six bottles.

| # | Quest | Area | How |
|---:|---|---|---|

| 2 | Goron Race | Goron Racetrack | <label><input type="checkbox"> Win after defeating Goht; the bottle initially contains Gold Dust.</label> |
| 3 | Romani's defense | Romani Ranch | <label><input type="checkbox"> Protect the barn from 2:00–5:30 AM on the First Night.</label> |
| 4 | Beaver Races | Zora Cape | <label><input type="checkbox"> Win the 20-ring and 25-ring courses.</label> |
| 5 | Dampé and Big Poe | Ikana Graveyard | <label><input type="checkbox"> Final Night: uncover three blue flames and defeat the Big Poe.</label> |
