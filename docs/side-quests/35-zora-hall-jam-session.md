## Route

1. Hookshot to the drummer's upper ledge and read his journal.
2. Play the written phrase with the bassist as Zora Link and remember the combined melody.
3. Remove the Zora Mask and play the full song behind Evan's piano.

> **Cycle note:** Evan only rewards human Link; as Mikau he treats the tune as band material rather than Link's composition.
