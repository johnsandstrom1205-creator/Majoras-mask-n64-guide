## Route

1. Hit every Deku Scrub and Guay target.
2. Watch the extreme edges and the tree for late spawns.
3. Reach at least 2,120 points for the full reward on N64.

> **Cycle note:** A perfect clear grants a time bonus. Missing a Guay behind the player is the common reason scores fall short.
