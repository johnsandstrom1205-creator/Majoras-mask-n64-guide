## Route

1. Shoot only red Octoroks.
2. Avoid every blue Octorok.
3. Clear all 50 red targets for the perfect reward.

> **Cycle note:** Learn spawn positions rather than sweeping the bow wildly; one blue hit ruins the perfect run.
