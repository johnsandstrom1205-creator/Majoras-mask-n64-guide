## Route

1. First Day, 10:00 AM–8:00 PM: obtain Kafei's Mask from Madame Aroma.
2. First Day, about 2:00 PM: speak to Anju after the Postman delivers her letter; wear Kafei's Mask and agree to meet.
3. First Night, 11:30 PM: enter the Stock Pot Inn kitchen and receive the Letter to Kafei.
4. Mail the letter before the next pickup.
5. Second Day, about 3:00 PM: follow the Postman to the Laundry Pool and enter Kafei's hideout after he collects the letter. Receive the Pendant of Memories.
6. Give the pendant to Anju before the Final Night.
7. Final Day, 1:00–10:00 PM: collect the Keaton Mask and Special Delivery from the Curiosity Shop owner in the hideout.
8. Final Day, about 6:00 PM: meet Kafei at Sakon's Hideout in Ikana. Alternate Link and Kafei to recover the Sun's Mask before it reaches the conveyor exit.
9. Final Night: choose one delivery branch for the Postman's Hat or Madame Aroma's Bottle. These require separate cycles for both rewards.
10. Return to the Stock Pot Inn employees-only room and wait until roughly 4:30 AM for the Couple's Mask.

> **Cycle note:** Do **not** stop Sakon's robbery on the First Night in the cycle used for the Sun's Mask; otherwise Sakon never brings it to his hideout.
