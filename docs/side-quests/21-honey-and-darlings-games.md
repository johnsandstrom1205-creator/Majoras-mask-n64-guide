## Route

1. Win the First Day Bombchu Gallery.
2. Win the Second Day Basket Bomb game.
3. Win the Final Day Target Shooting game.

> **Cycle note:** The reward requires three consecutive daily wins in one cycle. Slow time between sessions, not during a timed round.
