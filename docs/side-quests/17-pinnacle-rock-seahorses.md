## Route

1. Photograph a Gerudo pirate clearly.
2. Show the picture to the fisherman and bottle his Seahorse.
3. Release it between the offshore pillars and follow it through the murk.
4. Defeat all Deep Pythons and reunite the Seahorse pair.

> **Cycle note:** Save enough bottle space for the three Zora Eggs found in the snake dens, or plan return trips to the lab.
