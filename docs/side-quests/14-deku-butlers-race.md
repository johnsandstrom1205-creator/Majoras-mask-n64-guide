## Route

1. Enter the cave outside the palace after the royal reunion.
2. Follow the Butler's light through doors, moving walls, fire, and narrow platforms.
3. Switch between human and Deku form where their movement is strongest.
4. Reach the finish before he gives up waiting.

> **Cycle note:** Bunny Hood helps on foot, but remove it before precise jumps if the extra speed causes overshooting.
