## Route

1. Ride with Cremia from the ranch.
2. During the detour through Gorman Track, shoot the two masked pursuers repeatedly.
3. Keep at least one milk bottle intact until the wagon reaches Clock Town.

> **Cycle note:** The pursuers cannot be defeated. Alternate arrows between them; wearing the Circus Leader's Mask makes them refrain from attacking.
