## Route

1. Speak to the frog in Clock Town's Laundry Pool.
2. Speak to the frog on the log in Southern Swamp.
3. Defeat Gekko in Woodfall Temple and speak to the freed frog.
4. Defeat Gekko in Great Bay Temple and speak to that frog.
5. After Goht brings spring, wear Don Gero's Mask before the choir in Mountain Village.

> **Cycle note:** The four traveling frogs must be recruited in the same cycle as the final spring concert.
