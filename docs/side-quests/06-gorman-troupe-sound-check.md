## Route

1. Enter Latte and speak with Toto.
2. Stand under the requested light and perform as human Link.
3. Repeat as Deku, Goron, and Zora Link.
4. Let the completed arrangement play, then speak to Gorman.

> **Cycle note:** Each form adds a different instrument. You can switch masks inside the bar without restarting the event.
