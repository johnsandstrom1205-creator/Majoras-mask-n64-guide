## Route

1. Wait near the North Clock Town playground before midnight.
2. After Sakon steals the bag, strike him once with the sword.
3. Speak with the old woman after he flees.

> **Cycle note:** Never shoot Sakon with an arrow: the stolen sack contains explosives. For Anju/Kafei's Sun's Mask route, deliberately allow this robbery in that cycle.
