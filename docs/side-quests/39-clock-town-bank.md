## Route

1. Deposit spare rupees before every Song of Time reset.
2. Reach 200 total for the Adult Wallet.
3. Continue to 5,000 for the final heart piece.

> **Cycle note:** The balance is permanent. Fast farming options include the Termina Field Dodongo grotto and late-game Light Arrow enemy drops.
