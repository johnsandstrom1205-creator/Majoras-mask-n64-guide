## Route

1. Wear the Mask of Truth and pick up dogs to hear their confidence.
2. Choose a confident dog—gold is often favorable; avoid the black dog.
3. Bet 50 rupees and place first.

> **Cycle note:** The result still has randomness. Bank extra rupees and retry within the cycle if the best-sounding dog loses.
