## Route

1. Trade Moon's Tear to the South Clock Town scrub for the Land Title Deed.
2. As human Link, give Land Title Deed to the Southern Swamp scrub; use his flower for a Piece of Heart.
3. As Deku Link, trade the Swamp Title Deed to the Goron Village scrub; launch to another heart piece.
4. As Goron Link, trade the Mountain Title Deed to the scrub in Lulu's room at Zora Hall; use the flower for a heart piece.
5. As Zora Link, trade the Ocean Title Deed to the Ikana Canyon scrub; use the flower for the fourth heart piece.

> **Cycle note:** Title deeds are temporary and vanish on reset, so complete the chain in one cycle. The collected heart pieces remain.
