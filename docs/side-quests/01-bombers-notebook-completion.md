## Route

1. Complete the Bombers' membership event.
2. Finish Anju and Kafei's shared story.
3. Help the Curiosity Shop owner and Bomb Shop owner's mother.
4. Defend Romani and escort Cremia.
5. Resolve Mayor Dotour's meeting with the Couple's Mask.
6. Complete Madame Aroma, Toto, Gorman, Postman, Rosa Sisters, ???, Grandmother, Kamaro, Grog, Gorman Brothers, Shiro, and Guru-Guru entries.
