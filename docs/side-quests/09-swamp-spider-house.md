## Route

1. Burn the web over the entrance.
2. Kill all 30 Gold Skulltulas and collect every token.
3. Use bottled bugs on soft-soil patches; plant and water beans where required.
4. Listen for scratching to locate hidden Skulltulas.
5. Return to the cursed man with 30 tokens.

> **Cycle note:** Tokens reset with time, so all 30 must be collected in one cycle.
