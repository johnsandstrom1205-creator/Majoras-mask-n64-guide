## Route

1. Blast the Milk Road boulder before 6:00 PM on the First Day.
2. Complete Romani's Epona balloon practice.
3. Return before 2:00 AM and take a position near the barn.
4. Use Epona and the map to shoot every approaching alien until dawn.

> **Cycle note:** Play the Inverted Song of Time before the defense. Prioritize aliens nearest the barn; one also approaches from behind.
