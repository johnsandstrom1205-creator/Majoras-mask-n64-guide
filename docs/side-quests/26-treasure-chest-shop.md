## Route

1. Enter while wearing the Goron Mask.
2. Pay to play the randomized maze.
3. Use the overhead view and Goron speed to reach the chest before time expires.

> **Cycle note:** The prize depends on the form worn when paying. Enter as Goron Link for the heart piece.
