## Route

1. Bomb the cracked wall and enter the house.
2. Clear webs and Hookshot across the library and dining-room routes.
3. Collect all 30 Skulltula tokens in one cycle.
4. Wear Captain's Hat to question Stalchildren, then shoot the colored masks in their stated order for the heart piece.
5. Return to the entrance after all tokens.

> **Cycle note:** The N64 Giant Wallet reward is time-sensitive: complete the house before dawn of the Second Day.
