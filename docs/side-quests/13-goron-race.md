## Route

1. Open the racetrack with the Powder Keg test.
2. Enter the race as Goron Link.
3. Use rolling spikes, collect magic jars, and avoid being pinballed by the pack.
4. Pass on the final straight and finish first.

> **Cycle note:** Do not fight for first place immediately. Draft behind the pack, preserve magic, and make a controlled late pass.
