## Route

1. Catch fish in bottles from ponds or grottos.
2. Release them into the small two-fish aquarium near the lab entrance.
3. Keep feeding one side until the large fish eats the other and spits out the heart piece.

> **Cycle note:** Multiple bottles reduce travel; the fish count, not the number of visits, controls growth.
