## Route

1. On the First Day, pay the smiths 100 rupees to forge the Razor Sword overnight.
2. Win the Goron Race for a bottle of Gold Dust.
3. Collect the Razor Sword on the Second Day and immediately hand it back with Gold Dust.
4. Collect the Gilded Sword on the Final Day.

> **Cycle note:** The Razor Sword disappears after 100 strikes or a time reset. The Gilded Sword is permanent and does not lose sharpness.
