## Route

1. Swim west through Ikana's river as Zora Link and enter the cave.
2. Shoot the sun emblem with a Light Arrow.
3. Defeat the four miniboss rooms: Dinolfos, Wizzrobe, Wart, and Garo Master.
4. Return to the central attendant.

> **Cycle note:** Enter with at least 16 hearts to open all rooms in the N64 version. Bottled fairies and Chateau Romani make the gauntlet safer.
