## Route

1. Summon Epona inside the track.
2. Accept the two-on-one race.
3. Use carrots in measured bursts, take inside lines, and avoid every fence.
4. Cross the finish line first.

> **Cycle note:** Save one carrot for the final straight; fully exhausting the meter causes a longer recovery.
