## Route

1. First Night: order the Stalchildren to open the grave, defeat Iron Knuckle, and learn Song of Storms from Flat.
2. Second Night: open that night's grave, use Lens of Truth and bombs through the hidden route, then defeat Iron Knuckle for a Piece of Heart.
3. Final Night: guide Dampé to dirt patches until three blue flames appear; defeat the Big Poe for a Bottle.

> **Cycle note:** Each night guards a different grave. If Dampé refuses to move, Z-target him and walk slowly; use the brown platforms to lift him to upper soil patches.
