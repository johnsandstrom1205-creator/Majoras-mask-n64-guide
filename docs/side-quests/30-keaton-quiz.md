## Route

1. Wear the Keaton Mask near the moving bushes in North Clock Town or another Keaton circle.
2. Cut or lift the center bush.
3. Answer five randomly selected questions correctly.

> **Cycle note:** Questions test names, schedules, counts, and game facts. Resetting the conversation rerolls the set if you fail.
