## Normal temple

1. Use Elegy statues to hold the entrance switches and move the stone blocks.
2. Work through lava as Goron Link, water channels as Zora Link, and air currents as Deku Link.
3. Reflect sunlight with the Mirror Shield to remove sun blocks. Defeat Armos and Eyegores by striking their exposed eye after their attack.
4. Fight the Garo Master. Block the leap, counter when he lands, and take the **Light Arrows**.

## Invert the temple

Return outside, stand on the red emblem, and shoot the red jewel above the entrance with a Light Arrow. The entire temple flips.

In the inverted version, pits become ceilings and previously unreachable doors become routes. Light Arrows can flip marked rooms independently. Use Deku Flowers in updrafts, Hookshot chests and targets, and track whether each fairy chest must be opened before or after a room flips.

## Giant's Mask and Boss Key

Defeat the late Eyegore to receive the **Giant's Mask** in the N64 version. Continue through Wizzrobe and Gomess encounters, using Light Arrows to expose weak points. Obtain the Boss Key and return to the inverted boss entrance.

## Boss: Twinmold

Equip the Giant's Mask after entering the desert arena. It drains magic continuously.

- Slash each worm's head or tail as it emerges.
- Keep moving so the body does not collide with Link.
- Break pillars for magic jars when the meter runs low.
- Remove the mask briefly if you need to collect supplies efficiently.

Defeat both worms, collect the Heart Container, and take **Twinmold's Remains**.

## Great Fairy reward

Return all 15 Stone Tower fairies to the fountain in Ikana Canyon. The reward is the **Great Fairy's Sword**, a powerful C-button weapon.
