## Heal Mikau

Ride Epona over the fences west of Clock Town. At Great Bay Coast, swim behind the injured Zora in the water and push him to shore. Listen to Mikau, then play the **Song of Healing**. You receive the **Zora Mask**.

Zora Link swims rapidly, sinks and walks underwater, throws fin boomerangs, and creates an electric barrier while swimming at the cost of magic.

Activate the Great Bay Coast owl statue and visit the Marine Research Lab. The professor needs all seven stolen Zora Eggs placed in the aquarium.

## Pirates' Fortress: four eggs

Enter through the underwater opening in the fortress lagoon.

1. Manipulate underwater switches and currents to reach the inner courtyard.
2. In the guard tower, shoot the beehive through the window. The pirates flee the room below.
3. Open the chest there for the **Hookshot**.
4. Hookshot to wooden targets and enter four guarded rooms. Each contains one egg in a tank.
5. Defeat the Gerudo guards by blocking their lunges and countering. Bottle each egg.

The Stone Mask makes most patrol guards ignore you, but room guards still fight. If you have fewer than four empty bottles, deliver eggs to the lab between trips.

## Pinnacle Rock: three eggs

Photograph a Gerudo pirate and show it to the fisherman in the small coast hut. He lends you a Seahorse. Release it between the two offshore pillars and follow it through the murky water.

At Pinnacle Rock, defeat all Deep Pythons using Zora electric attacks. Three dens contain the remaining eggs; the reunited Seahorses also award a Piece of Heart.

## Learn New Wave Bossa Nova

Deposit all seven eggs in the lab aquarium. Stand where you can see the hatched Zora larvae and take out the Zora guitar. Their formation teaches **New Wave Bossa Nova**.

Play the song for Lulu at Zora Cape. Her voice returns, the giant turtle awakens, and it carries you to Great Bay Temple.
