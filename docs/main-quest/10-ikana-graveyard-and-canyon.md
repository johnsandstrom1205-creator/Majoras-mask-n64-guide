## Required masks before Ikana

- Win the Gorman Brothers' horse race on Milk Road for **Garo's Mask**.
- On the Road to Ikana, use the Lens of Truth at the ring of stones to reveal Shiro. Give him Red Potion for the **Stone Mask**.
- In Ikana Graveyard, play the Sonata of Awakening for Skull Keeta, chase him, stun him with arrows, and defeat him for the **Captain's Hat**.

## Graveyard, First Night

Wear the Captain's Hat and order the Stalchildren to open the First Night grave. Inside, defeat the enemies and light the torches. Defeat the Iron Knuckle and learn the **Song of Storms** from Flat.

The Second Night grave contains a Piece of Heart; the Final Night grave contains the N64 bottle quest with Dampé. Both are optional but count toward 100%.

## Enter Ikana Canyon

Ride Epona over the eastern fences. Wear Garo's Mask near the hooded cliff watcher; he creates a tree. Hookshot upward, then make ice platforms across the river and Hookshot through the canyon. Activate the owl statue.

## Restore the Music Box House

Follow the dry river into the cave and defeat the Sharp encounter by playing the **Song of Storms**. Water returns, starting the Music Box House and driving away the surrounding Gibdos.

Wait until Pamela leaves the house, or use the Stone Mask to approach unnoticed. Enter, go downstairs, and approach the wardrobe. Play the **Song of Healing** for Pamela's transformed father to receive the **Gibdo Mask**.

The Gibdo Mask makes Gibdos speak rather than attack. It is the key to the trading doors Beneath the Well.
