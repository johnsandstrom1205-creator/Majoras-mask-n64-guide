## Slow the clock first

Play the Song of Time backwards — **Down-C, A, Right-C, Down-C, A, Right-C** on the N64 layout — to play the Inverted Song of Time. Time moves at roughly one-third speed, which is the safest setting for exploration and dungeons. Play the inverted song again to restore normal speed.

## Obtain the Bombers' Notebook

Return to human form, pop Jim's balloon in North Clock Town, and complete the Bombers' hide-and-seek game again. Human Link is admitted as a member and receives the **Bombers' Notebook**. You may also use the code from the first cycle at the East Clock Town passage; Jim will stop you afterward and give you the notebook.

The notebook records scheduled people and rewards, but it does not make events permanent. A filled blue bar means you completed that event during the current cycle; a happiness seal records a permanent completion.

## Obtain the Great Fairy's Mask

Find Clock Town's Stray Fairy again and return it to the North Clock Town fountain as human Link. The Great Fairy gives you the **Great Fairy's Mask**, which shimmers when an uncollected Stray Fairy is in the current dungeon room and pulls free fairies toward you.

## Recommended early purchases

- Buy the **Bomb Bag** for 50 rupees in the West Clock Town Bomb Shop.
- Deposit a total of 200 rupees for the **Adult Wallet**.
- Buy the Clock Town and Woodfall maps from Tingle when convenient; maps are optional but permanent.
- Learn a Scarecrow's Song only when you plan to use it in the same cycle. The custom song resets with time.

## Open the four regions

Human Link can leave through Clock Town's gates after speaking to a guard. The intended order is south to Woodfall, north to Snowhead, west to Great Bay, and east to Ikana. Items from one region unlock the next.

## Useful time controls

- **Song of Double Time:** play each Song of Time note twice to jump to the next dawn or night on N64.
- **Owl saves:** striking an owl statue makes a temporary suspend save. Loading it deletes that owl save; it is not a replacement for returning to the First Day.
- **Banking:** deposit rupees before every reset. The banker identifies you by the stamp he places on Link, so the balance survives time travel.
