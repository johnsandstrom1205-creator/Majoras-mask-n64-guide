## How the temple works

Snowhead is built around a central stone column. Goron punches remove blue segments and lower the route; striking red switches raises parts of it. Avoid destroying a segment until you have used the ledge it supports.

## Route to the Fire Arrows

1. Break the entrance icicles and push the block to open the first routes.
2. In the bridge room, roll as Goron Link across the gap. Explore the lower floor for keys and the map before committing upward.
3. Use bombs on cracked walls and the Lens of Truth on suspicious ice patches. Several fairy chests sit behind invisible walls or platforms.
4. Goron Pound the large switches to raise temporary platforms. Move quickly before they retract.
5. Fight **Wizzrobe** by watching which tile becomes solid. Shoot or strike him before his spell fires. The reward is **Fire Arrows**.

## Raise and climb the central pillar

Use Fire Arrows to melt frozen doors, blocks, and eye switches. Work around the colored routes, collecting the Boss Key and opening paths back to the central chamber. When the pillar is correctly raised, climb the outer ledges. Punch out only the blue discs that block your upward route.

The final ascent has narrow snow ramps. Build Goron roll speed on the flat section, release the stick slightly to stay centered, and cross each gap with magic spikes active.

## Boss: Goht

Melt the ice around Goht with a Fire Arrow. Transform into Goron Link and chase.

- Roll until spikes appear, then ram Goht's legs or flank.
- Avoid lightning, falling rocks, bombs, and the barriers Goht kicks backward.
- Use green jars around the track to refill magic without stopping.
- When Goht falls, stop rolling and land punches or another rolling strike.

Collect the Heart Container and **Goht's Remains**. The mountain becomes spring for the rest of the current cycle.

## Great Fairy reward

Return all 15 Snowhead fairies to the fountain at the base of Snowhead for a doubled magic meter.
