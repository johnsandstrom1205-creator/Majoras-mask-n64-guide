## Prepare for the well

Wear the Gibdo Mask at every mummy door. Different Gibdos request consumables. A practical supply is:

- 5 Magic Beans, 10 Deku Nuts, 10 Bombs
- one Fish and one bottle of Bugs
- Milk, Blue Potion, and Hot Spring Water for optional branches
- a Poe or Big Poe for deeper optional rooms

You do not need every optional reward to reach the Mirror Shield, but collecting supplies first prevents repeated trips. The well contains sources for some requested items; inspect jars, pools, and enemies before leaving.

## Reach the Mirror Shield

Trade through the central route while wearing the Gibdo Mask. Use Fire Arrows on webs and torches, bottles for live items, and the Lens of Truth where the walls look suspicious. The final chamber contains light. Reflect it onto sun emblems and sun blocks to open the chest holding the **Mirror Shield**.

Shine the shield's reflected light on the sun block to exit directly into the Ancient Castle of Ikana.

## Clear Ikana Castle

1. Use the Mirror Shield to remove sun blocks and defeat ReDeads safely with the Gibdo Mask equipped.
2. Hit crystal switches and use Deku Flowers across the roof. A roof chest contains an optional Piece of Heart.
3. Use a Powder Keg on the cracked roof to let sunlight into the throne-room route.
4. Fight the two skeletal guards. Burn away their curtains with Fire Arrows and expose their remains to light so they cannot revive.
5. Fight King Igos du Ikana. Reflect light onto him after knocking him down; block or sidestep his heavy attacks.

After the battle, the king teaches the **Elegy of Emptiness**. The song creates a statue of the form currently playing it. Human, Goron, and Zora statues hold heavy floor switches; the Deku statue is too light for most large switches.
