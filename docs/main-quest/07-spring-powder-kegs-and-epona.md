## Powder Keg certification

In spring, enter the Powder Keg Shop in Goron Village. Carry the test keg to the blocked Goron Racetrack entrance and place it beside the boulder before its fuse expires. Return to the shop after the explosion. You may now buy and carry Powder Kegs as Goron Link, including from the Bomb Shop in Clock Town.

## Goron Race and Gold Dust

Enter the racetrack and win. Stay just behind the leaders until the course opens, conserve magic, and use the final straight to pass. The reward is a bottle of **Gold Dust**.

For the permanent **Gilded Sword**, bring Gold Dust to the Mountain Smithy after they forge the temporary Razor Sword. The full process spans nights, so start on the First Day and see the Sword Upgrades side quest before resetting.

## Reclaim Epona

Use a Powder Keg on Milk Road's boulder before 6:00 PM on the First Day. At Romani Ranch:

1. Speak to Romani near the barn.
2. Complete her balloon target practice while riding Epona.
3. Learn **Epona's Song** and permanently regain the ability to summon Epona in outdoor areas.

Epona can jump the fences to Great Bay and Ikana and lets you race the Gorman Brothers for Garo's Mask.

## Optional ranch continuation

Return before 2:00 AM on the First Night to defend the barn. Completing that defense earns another bottle and unlocks Cremia's Second Day escort, which awards Romani's Mask.

## What persists after reset

Powder Keg certification, Epona's Song, the bottle, Gold Dust bottle slot, Gilded Sword, and masks persist. Spring itself does not; replay Goht from the temple entrance whenever you need the thawed region in a later cycle.
