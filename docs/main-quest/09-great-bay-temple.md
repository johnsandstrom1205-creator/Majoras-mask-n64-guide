## Read the pipe colors

Great Bay Temple is a pump station. **Red pipes** are activated in the first half; after reversing the central current, **green pipes** carry you through the second half. Trace a pipe from a locked mechanism back to its valve instead of exploring randomly.

## Activate the red system

1. In the entrance room, use Hookshot targets and the elevator platforms to reach the first valve.
2. Enter the central turbine chamber. Swim with the current and use side passages rather than fighting it head-on.
3. Explore the red-pipe rooms for the map, compass, Small Keys, and valves. Zora Link can sink beneath spinning machinery and use fins to break jars.
4. Defeat **Wart**: shoot its eye to loosen bubbles, destroy the loose bubbles, then attack the exposed eye. The chest contains **Ice Arrows**.

## Reverse the flow

Ice Arrows create temporary platforms only on the sparkling water surfaces used by the N64 puzzle. Use them to reach valves and to freeze waterfalls or waterwheels. Fire Arrows thaw frozen mechanisms.

Once the red route has powered the main mechanism, turn the central valve to reverse the turbine. The new current carries you into green-pipe rooms.

## Complete the green system

Use ice platforms, seesaws, Hookshot targets, and heated/frozen water to reach the remaining green valves. When all green flow reaches the final chamber, the Boss Door route opens.

## Boss: Gyorg

Stand on the central platform and stun Gyorg with an arrow, Hookshot, or Zora fins. Dive as Zora Link and strike with the electric barrier, then return to the platform before Gyorg recovers.

Later, Gyorg releases smaller fish. Clear them with the barrier and keep moving. Never remain at the platform edge when Gyorg charges; it can knock Link into the water and chain attacks.

## Great Fairy reward

Return all 15 Great Bay fairies to the fountain on the cliff islands near Zora Cape. Use the Hookshot to cross. The reward is doubled defense: enemy damage is halved.
