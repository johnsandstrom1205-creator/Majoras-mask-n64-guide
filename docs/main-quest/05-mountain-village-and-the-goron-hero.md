## Enter the frozen north

Leave Clock Town through the north gate. Shoot the large icicle above the blocked path twice; it falls and breaks the ice below. Continue through Mountain Village and activate its owl statue.

## Obtain the Lens of Truth

At Goron Village, speak to the owl. He flies across the gap, dropping feathers over invisible platforms. Follow the feather trail to the cave shrine and open the chest for the **Lens of Truth**. Use it to see the platforms on the return trip.

Back in Goron Village, use the Lens to see Darmani's ghost and follow him to Mountain Village. Climb the invisible ladder on the cliff wall to reach Goron Graveyard.

## Heal Darmani

Speak with Darmani and play the **Song of Healing**. You receive the **Goron Mask**. Goron Link can roll at high speed, punch heavy objects, walk through lava, and Goron Pound switches.

Pull the gravestone from behind to release Hot Spring Water. Bottle it quickly—it cools into ordinary water after a short time.

## Complete the Goron Lullaby

1. Find the frozen Goron Elder on the route between Mountain Village and Goron Village. His position changes with the day.
2. Thaw him with Hot Spring Water and speak to him as Goron Link. Learn the **Lullaby Intro**.
3. Goron Pound the shrine entrance in Goron Village and play the intro for the crying baby.
4. The baby completes the song, giving you the **Goron Lullaby**.

## Climb Snowhead

Roll across the ramps on the Snowhead path, using magic spikes to clear long gaps. At the summit, the Lens of Truth reveals a giant Goron blowing you backward. Play the Goron Lullaby to put him to sleep, then climb to the temple entrance and activate the owl statue.

> **Recommended detour:** light every torch in the Goron Shrine to spin the chandelier, break its pots with a rolling jump, and deliver the Rock Sirloin to the hungry Goron for Don Gero's Mask.
