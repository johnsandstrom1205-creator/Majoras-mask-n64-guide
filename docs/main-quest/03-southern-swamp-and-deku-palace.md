## Reach Southern Swamp

Leave Clock Town through the south gate. On the Road to Southern Swamp, climb the vine-covered tree for an optional Piece of Heart and shoot down Tingle if you want the Woodfall map.

At the swamp, activate the owl statue near the Tourist Center. The poisoned water hurts human Link, but Deku Link can hop across its surface five times before sinking.

## Rescue Koume

1. Cross the lily pads beside the Tourist Center to reach the Magic Hags' Potion Shop.
2. Enter the Woods of Mystery and follow the monkey. The correct turns change by day, so keep the monkey in sight rather than memorizing one route.
3. Speak to injured Koume, return to Kotake, and receive a bottle containing Red Potion.
4. Bring the potion to Koume. Keep the now-empty bottle permanently.

Return to the Tourist Center and take Koume's boat cruise. The boat knocks away the Big Octo blocking the poison swamp route. Disembark at Deku Palace and receive the **Pictograph Box**.

## Infiltrate Deku Palace

The king has imprisoned a monkey for the missing princess. Guards will throw you out if they see you.

1. Enter the palace as Deku Link and go to the throne room to learn what happened.
2. Use the side garden route. Move only when the guards look away; flowers and walls provide cover.
3. Buy a Magic Bean from the underground bean seller if needed, plant it in the soft soil outside, and water it with Spring Water from the nearby grotto.
4. Ride the plant to the upper route, then use Deku Flowers to cross above the guards.
5. Reach the monkey's cage and play the Ocarina. The monkey teaches the **Sonata of Awakening**.

## Raise Woodfall Temple

Follow the monkey across the poisonous swamp to Woodfall. At the stone monument, meet the owl and learn the **Song of Soaring**. Activate the Woodfall owl statue.

Use Deku Flowers around the outer ring to reach the central platform. Play the Sonata of Awakening as Deku Link. Woodfall Temple rises from the swamp.

## Cycle safety

If time is already late on the Second Day, activate the owl and reset before entering the temple. The Sonata, bottle, Pictograph Box, and owl activation survive. Starting Woodfall Temple early gives enough time to collect all 15 Stray Fairies.
