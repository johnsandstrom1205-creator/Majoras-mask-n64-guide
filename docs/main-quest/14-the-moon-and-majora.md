## Prepare the final cycle

The ending only requires the four Boss Remains. For 100%, first finish every permanent reward you want, fill bottles with fairies or Chateau Romani, stock arrows and magic, and bring all 23 other masks.

At midnight on the Final Day, climb the Clock Tower and play the **Oath to Order**. The Four Giants stop the moon. Follow Skull Kid to the moon.

## The four Moon trials

The children wearing the four boss masks each request normal masks and open a themed mini-dungeon. Across all four trials, surrender all 20 non-transformation masks. Each trial contains one Piece of Heart.

- **Odolwa trial:** Deku Flower precision. Control momentum by releasing the stick while gliding.
- **Goht trial:** Goron rolling track. Enter ramps straight and avoid steering sharply at full speed.
- **Gyorg trial:** Zora current maze. In the N64 version, choose branches carefully; wrong routes loop or eject you.
- **Twinmold trial:** combat gauntlet and hidden switches. Bring bombs, arrows, and magic.

After clearing the trials and giving away all eligible masks, speak to the child wearing Majora's Mask under the tree. He gives you the **Fierce Deity's Mask**.

## Final boss

Enter with the child when ready.

### Majora's Mask

Stun the mask from the front with arrows, Zora fins, or reflected projectiles, then strike its back. The four Boss Remains eventually animate; destroy them while continuing to watch Majora.

### Majora's Incarnation

The boss runs unpredictably. Keep it targeted, use arrows to create openings, and punish knockdowns. Do not waste time chasing without a stun.

### Majora's Wrath

Block or sidestep the whips, close the distance after a combo, and attack. Light Arrows create reliable openings. Fierce Deity turns the battle into a much easier ranged sword fight but is optional.

After victory, the cycle ends and the credits reflect characters whose notebook stories you completed.
