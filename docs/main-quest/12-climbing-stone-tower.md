## The statue rule

The Elegy of Emptiness leaves one statue for each playable form. Playing it again in the same form moves that form's statue. Use human, Goron, and Zora statues on large switches; use Deku Link mainly for movement and small switches.

## Ascend the tower

1. Start at the lower Stone Tower entrance in Ikana Canyon.
2. Step on the first switch and play the Elegy. Cross to the platform that moved into place.
3. Change forms and repeat on the next switches. Watch which stone blocks rotate or slide before leaving a statue.
4. Use Hookshot targets for vertical moves and Deku Flowers for long gaps.
5. If the route becomes impossible, return to the most recent switch and replace the statue in the correct form. You never need to reset the entire climb.

At the top, activate the **Stone Tower owl statue** immediately. This preserves a fast route even if you return to the First Day.

## Open the temple

Use Elegy statues on the three entrance switches to align the giant blocks, then cross into Stone Tower Temple.

> **100% planning:** enter with the Great Fairy's Mask, a full magic meter, arrows, bombs, and at least one fairy in a bottle. Stone Tower's 15 fairies require visiting rooms in both normal and inverted orientations.
