## Temple route

Wear the Great Fairy's Mask often if you are collecting all 15 Stray Fairies. Fairies return to the temple when time resets, even after the boss is defeated.

1. **Entrance platforms:** use Deku Flowers to cross the first room. Open the obvious chest and clear enemies for early fairies.
2. **Poison chamber:** descend using the flowers, defeat the Deku Baba on the ramp, and collect the Small Key from the side platform.
3. **Push-block room:** move the block, carry a lit Deku Stick around the loop, and light the unlit torch to open the way upward.
4. **Dark room:** defeat every Black Boe, then light the torches. Cross as Deku Link.
5. **Dinolfos:** defeat the miniboss with shielded sword attacks and receive the **Hero's Bow**.
6. **Central mechanism:** shoot the yellow eye to raise a platform. Later, light the central torch with an arrow through fire to purify the main flower mechanism and stop the poisoned flow.
7. **Gekko and Snapper:** stun the Snapper by diving into a flower and launching beneath it. When Gekko rides it, repeat; shoot Gekko with arrows whenever he climbs the walls. Take the Boss Key.
8. **Final flower chamber:** shoot Dragonflies, then use flowers and moving platforms to reach the Boss Door. Several fairies are hidden in bubbles in the wall alcoves.

## Boss: Odolwa

Keep Odolwa targeted and punish the end of his sword strings with jump attacks. Arrows can interrupt chants and bring down bugs; bombs and Bomb Flowers clear summoned enemies. Deku Flowers provide safe launches if the arena becomes crowded. Do not chase blindly—block, wait for a committed swing, then counter.

Collect the Heart Container and **Odolwa's Remains**. You also learn the **Oath to Order** from the freed Giant.

## Rescue the princess

After the boss, cut the vines in the small room and put the Deku Princess in an empty bottle. Return her to the Deku King. This clears the swamp's story state for the current cycle and unlocks the Deku Butler race for the Mask of Scents.

## Great Fairy reward

With all 15 Woodfall fairies, visit the fountain in a cave along Woodfall's outer wall. The restored Great Fairy grants an enhanced spin attack.
