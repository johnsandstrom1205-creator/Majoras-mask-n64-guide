## Goal

You begin trapped as a Deku Scrub. Your permanent objective is to recover Majora's Mask, but this first cycle teaches the rules that every later quest uses: schedules reset, key items survive, and the clock is always part of the puzzle.

## Reach Clock Town

1. Chase Skull Kid through the forest tunnels. Use Deku Flowers to cross gaps and open the chest of Deku Nuts on the way.
2. After meeting the Happy Mask Salesman beneath the Clock Tower, enter South Clock Town. Tatl points out that the Clock Tower opens at midnight on the Final Day.
3. Activate the owl statue in South Clock Town by striking it. Owl statues become permanent warp points after you later learn the Song of Soaring.

## Restore the Great Fairy

The Great Fairy Fountain is in North Clock Town. Her missing Stray Fairy changes location with the time of day:

- **Daytime:** Laundry Pool.
- **Night:** East Clock Town, floating near the Stock Pot Inn/Treasure Chest Shop area.

Bring the fairy to the fountain. As Deku Link you receive magic power and can fire bubbles.

## Learn the Bombers' code

1. Pop Jim's balloon in North Clock Town with a bubble.
2. Agree to the Bombers' hide-and-seek game and catch all five before the next dawn. Two are in North Clock Town, two in East Clock Town, and one in West Clock Town.
3. Write down the five-digit code. It is randomized for each save file but remains the same after time resets.
4. Give the code to the Bomber in East Clock Town and follow the underground passage to the Astral Observatory.

## Obtain the Moon's Tear

At the observatory, look through the telescope and zoom in on Skull Kid at the top of the Clock Tower. Pick up the Moon's Tear that falls outside. Trade it to the Business Scrub in South Clock Town for the Land Title Deed and access to his Deku Flower.

Use the flower to reach the Clock Tower ledge. The door itself will not open until midnight on the Final Day, so use the remaining time to practice movement, collect rupees, or try early Clock Town activities. Deposit spare rupees at the bank before the cycle ends.

## Confront Skull Kid

At midnight on the Final Day, the Clock Tower opens. Launch from the nearby flower and climb to the roof.

1. Target Skull Kid and hit him with a bubble.
2. Pick up the Ocarina of Time.
3. Play the **Song of Time** when prompted. You return to Dawn of the First Day.
4. Enter the lower Clock Tower and speak to the Happy Mask Salesman.
5. Play the **Song of Healing** to separate the Deku spirit from Link and receive the **Deku Mask**.

> **Reset rule:** Rupees in your wallet, ammunition, bottled consumables, temporary quest items, and most scheduled events reset. Masks, songs, equipment, heart pieces, bottles, upgrades, dungeon remains, maps, and banked rupees remain.
